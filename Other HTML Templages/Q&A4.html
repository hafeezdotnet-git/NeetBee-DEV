QUESTION 01 ⚡ Physics
A body starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2 m/s². What will be its velocity after 5 seconds?
A 5 m/s
B 10 m/s ✓
C 15 m/s
D 20 m/s
✓ CORRECT ANSWER
B — 10 m/s
Using v = u + at, the velocity is 0 + (2 × 5) = 10 m/s.
QUESTION 02 🧪 Chemistry
What is the chemical formula of water?
A CO₂
B H₂O ✓
C O₂
D NaCl
✓ CORRECT ANSWER
B — H₂O
Water contains two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.
QUESTION 03 🧬 Biology
Which organelle is known as the powerhouse of the cell?
A Nucleus
B Mitochondria ✓
C Ribosome
D Golgi apparatus
✓ CORRECT ANSWER
B — Mitochondria
Mitochondria produce ATP through cellular respiration and are commonly called the powerhouse of the cell.
QUESTION 04 ➗ Mathematics
If x + 5 = 12, what is the value of x?
A 5
B 7 ✓
C 8
D 12
✓ CORRECT ANSWER
B — 7
Subtract 5 from both sides: x = 12 minus 5, which equals 7.
