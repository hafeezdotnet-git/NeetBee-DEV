Q1 ⚡ Physics ✓ Correct
A body starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2 m/s². What will be its velocity after 5 seconds?
A 5 m/s
B 10 m/s
C 15 m/s
D 20 m/s
YOUR ANSWER B — 10 m/s
CORRECT ANSWER B — 10 m/s
💡 Explanation

Using v = u + at, where u = 0, a = 2 m/s² and t = 5 seconds, the velocity is 10 m/s.
